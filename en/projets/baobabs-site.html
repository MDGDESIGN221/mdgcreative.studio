Website

Baobabs Basket Club

The website of a basketball club founded in Dakar in 2026. Fourteen sections built around a season: club news, the roster player by player, media, the shop, the app, the head-to-head stats between the two leaders, tryouts and ticketing. The club's visual identity, designed beforehand, holds its line throughout, from deep green to lime.
