Sokan Business

The site of a freight forwarder moving goods from China, Europe and the rest of the world to Dakar. The trade is technical: the page owns it and equips it. A chargeable-volume calculator that says whether groupage or a full container fits, an Incoterms table that settles who pays what and how far, tracking by file number, four costed corridors. Black and aqua, a massive display type, to hold eleven sections without losing the reader.
