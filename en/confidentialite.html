Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: February 2026

At MDG Creative Studio, privacy is taken seriously. This page explains, in plain terms, what data is collected, why, how long it is kept, and what your rights are. This processing complies with Senegalese law no. 2008-12 of 25 January 2008 on the protection of personal data.

01 Data controller

The data controller is Mouhamed Al Amine Diawara (MDG Creative Studio), Kaolack, Senegal. Contact: mdgdesign221@gmail.com or WhatsApp on +221 76 377 22 08.

02 Data collected

This site only collects data when you choose to contact us through one of the forms. No sign-up, no account, no advertising profiling.

When you fill in a form (project enquiry, pack booking), the following is collected:

  • Your name or artist name;
  • Your contact details (email, WhatsApp number, or Instagram);
  • The project information you freely provide (type of work, message, indicative budget).

03 Purpose & legal basis

This data is used solely to get back to you and to prepare a quote or start a project. The legal basis is your consent, given by voluntarily submitting the form. It is never sold, rented, or used for advertising.

04 Processors & third-party tools

To operate, the site relies on a few trusted third-party services:

Web3Forms
Forwards form content to our inbox. The data passes through their servers for the time it takes to send. Web3Forms policy
Vercel
Site hosting and anonymous audience measurement (Vercel Analytics, with no cookie and no identifying data). Vercel policy
Google Fonts
Loads the typefaces; may pass your IP address to Google. Google policy

When you click through to WhatsApp, Instagram, Behance, TikTok or Spotify, you leave this site and come under the privacy policy of the platform concerned.

05 Cookies

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no marketing tool. Audience measurement (Vercel Analytics) works without cookies: no identifying data is collected. Our host Vercel sets no tracking cookie.

06 Retention period

Messages received through the forms are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, then for no more than 3 years after our last exchange, for follow-up purposes. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

07 Your rights

Under the law, you have the right to access, correct, object to and delete your data. To exercise it, write to mdgdesign221@gmail.com. You may also refer the matter to Senegal's Personal Data Protection Commission (CDP): www.cdp.sn.

08 Security

The site is served exclusively over HTTPS, protected by Vercel, with hardened security headers. Your exchanges are encrypted in transit.
